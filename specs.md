## 1. Project name and overview

### Project name

JanSetu

### Overview

This track focuses on democratizing information, modernizing public service delivery, and bridging the gap between citizens, regulatory policy, and public institutions. In an era of rapid legislative changes, civic movements, digital infrastructure growth, and viral misinformation, citizens struggle to access verified facts or navigate public services, while businesses face complex regulatory compliance hurdles.

Solutions in this track leverage a mix of generative AI, multimodal data processing, browser-use automation, and autonomous workflows to digest legal notifications, verify media credibility, streamline bureaucratic processes, and build accessible citizen tools.

## 2. Problem statement

### Problem

millions of everyday citizens—especially small farmers, street vendors, daily-wage workers, senior citizens, and first-generation learners—are locked out of the public welfare and legal protections meant for them due to three interconnected barriers:
The "Bureaucratic Language & Literacy Wall" (Information Asymmetry)
Government notifications, welfare circulars, ration/pension orders, and municipal laws are published in dense legal English or formal administrative prose.
Real-World Consequence: A citizen who cannot read complex legalese—or who receives a confusing government notice or rejection slip—either misses out on thousands of rupees in rightful entitlements (like PM-KISAN, PM SVANidhi, Ayushman Bharat ₹5L Health Cover, or e-Shram accident protection) or is forced to pay middlemen and touts just to understand what a paper says.
Viral Misinformation, Phishing Scams & Panic Forwards
Low-trust and first-time internet users are disproportionately targeted by viral WhatsApp forwards, lookalike scam links (e.g., .xyz domains promising "Instant ₹15,000 PM Welfare Cash"), and doctored circulars claiming ration cards or pensions are being canceled overnight.
Real-World Consequence: Citizens have no instant, accessible way to cross-check whether a viral message or screenshot is a real government notification (.gov.in / PIB / Gazette) or a phishing trap before clicking or forwarding it.
Lack of Accessible, Last-Mile Legal & RTI Guidance
When a legitimate benefit is delayed, a ration dealer refuses portability under One Nation One Ration Card, or an application is stuck, ordinary citizens rarely know how to invoke statutory remedies like Section 6(1) & Section 7(1) of the Right to Information (RTI) Act, 2005 or State Public Service Guarantee Acts.

## 6. Processing rules and business logic

### Processing logic

JanSetu transforms raw citizen inputs into actionable results using a hybrid two-layer architecture: a Deterministic Statutory Rule Engine (src/utils/civicLogic.ts) that guarantees zero-hallucination math and legal accuracy, paired with a Server-Side Multimodal Gemini Flash & Grounding Pipeline (server.ts) for natural language translation, document OCR, and live fact verification.
1. Deterministic Scheme Eligibility & Entitlement Scoring (evaluateSchemeEligibility)
When a citizen selects a work persona or adjusts their age, income, or state, the system evaluates their profile against every scheme in the statutory registry using a 100-point weighted scoring algorithm:
Occupation & Special Category Matching (45 Points):
Checks whether the citizen's occupation (Farmer, Street Vendor, Daily Worker, Woman Entrepreneur, Student, Senior Citizen) matches the scheme's target beneficiary list.
Statutory Override Rule: Under the Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY Senior Citizen Expansion, if a citizen's age is 70 or above, the rule engine automatically grants the full 45 occupation points and waives the income ceiling—mirroring the actual Government of India notification.
Household Income Threshold Rule (30 Points):
Compares the citizen's annual family income against each scheme's statutory cap (e.g., ₹2,50,000 for PM-KISAN, ₹2,40,000 for e-Shram, ₹3,50,000 for NSP Scholarships). If within the limit (or exempt via the 70+ senior rule), 30 points are awarded; otherwise, the exact income gap is logged under missingRequirements.
Statutory Age Window Rule (15 Points):
Validates minimum and maximum age bounds (e.g., 16–59 years for e-Shram unorganised workers, 14–30 years for Post-Matric Scholarships, 18+ years for PM SVANidhi).
Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) Readiness Check (10 Points):
Checks whether the citizen has an active bank account for Aadhaar-linked Direct Benefit Transfer.
Aggregation & Ranking:
Schemes with zero missing statutory requirements (or a score 
 where only a zero-balance Jan Dhan account needs to be opened) are marked isEligible = true.
The engine sums the annualBenefitINR of all eligible schemes to display the citizen's Total Yearly Entitlement Value (₹/year) and sorts results by highest match score first, then by highest monetary benefit.
2. Multimodal Notice & Circular De-Jargoning Pipeline (POST /api/civic/explain-document)
When a user uploads a photo of a government circular or pastes legal text, the backend transforms it through four processing stages:
Multimodal Ingestion:
Strips any data-URL prefix from uploaded images and packages the raw Base64 bytes (inlineData) alongside the text prompt for Gemini Flash (gemini-3.8-flash) with ThinkingLevel.HIGH.
Reading-Level Conditioning:
If the user selects Simple Everyday Words, the system instruction enforces village/neighborhood vocabulary and bans bureaucratic jargon.
If the user selects Detailed Legal Clauses, the system instruction requires exact section numbers, Gazette references, and administrative appeal timelines.
Strict Structured Schema Extraction (responseSchema):
Instead of returning an unstructured wall of text, the model is constrained by a strict JSON schema to extract six mandatory fields: documentTitle, twoSentencePlainSummary, whatYouGetOrOwe, whoIsEligibleOrAffected[], threeActionSteps[], and deadlineOrWarning (which explicitly highlights official fees such as ₹0 to prevent middleman extortion).
Resilient Statutory Fallback:
If network connectivity drops or API quota is exhausted, a deterministic statutory parser inspects the notice for domain signatures (PM-KISAN/Land Seeding, Street Vendor/SVANidhi, RTI Section 7/19) and returns verified step-by-step remedies so the citizen is never left with an error screen.
3. Two-Stage Misinformation & Phishing Audit Logic (SatyaShield)
When a viral WhatsApp forward, link, or screenshot enters Satya Fact-Check, the system runs a two-stage verification pipeline:
Stage A — Deterministic Phishing & Scam Heuristics (evaluateClaimCredibilityHeuristics):
Scans the text using regex pattern rules to compute a Suspicion Score (0–98):
Adds +35 points if viral chain-forwarding bait is detected ("Forward to 10 groups", "share immediately", "last chance tonight").
Adds +30 points if unverified universal cash promises are detected ("free ₹15,000 instant cash transfer to all").
Adds +35 points if shortened or lookalike scam domains are detected (bit.ly, t.me, .xyz, .top, -gov.in).
Subtracts 10 points (down to a minimum baseline) only when a genuine .gov.in, .nic.in, or rbi.org.in domain is present without lookalike links.
Classifies the preliminary risk into HIGH_RISK_SCAM_OR_RUMOR (
), CONTAINS_OFFICIAL_REFERENCE (
 with .gov.in), or NEEDS_OFFICIAL_CROSS_CHECK.
Stage B — Live Search Grounding & MediaWiki Cross-Verification (POST /api/civic/fact-check):
Extracts key policy terms and queries the live Wikipedia / MediaWiki Action API (en.wikipedia.org/w/api.php) for matching statutory acts and welfare schemes.
Invokes Gemini Flash (gemini-3.8-flash) with Google Search Grounding ({ googleSearch: {} }) and the Stage A heuristic flags to cross-check the claim against live web sources, extract verified citation URLs (groundingChunks), and produce a final Truth Score (0–100) and verdict (DOCTORED_OR_SCAM, MISLEADING_CONTEXT, or VERIFIED_FACT).
4. Role-Conditioned Multi-Turn Legal Reasoning (POST /api/civic/chat)
When a citizen interacts with JanSahayak AI Chat, the system transforms their query based on three processing rules:
Role Persona Injection:
Applies a specialized systemInstruction depending on the active role:
Gram & Ward Sahayak: Formats every answer into warm, jargon-free 1-2-3 steps.
Constitutional & RTI Legal Counsel: Applies Right to Information Act, 2005 rules—specifically enforcing the 30-day statutory reply limit under Section 7(1), the 48-hour Life & Liberty Proviso, the ₹0 BPL fee exemption, free information supply after delay under Section 7(6), and the ₹250/day penalty (up to ₹25,000) under Section 20.
Forensic Rumor Auditor: Focuses on URL provenance, PIB Fact Check verification, and scam link detection.
Dynamic Reasoning Depth (thinkingLevel):
Maps the user's selected mode to Gemini's reasoning configuration: ThinkingLevel.LOW for Fast Flash Lookup, ThinkingLevel.MEDIUM for Standard Flash Guide, and ThinkingLevel.HIGH whenever Deep Flash Thinking is selected or a photo is attached.
Voice Synthesis Transformation (POST /api/civic/tts):
Any output across the entire app can be passed to speakCivicText(), which requests a 24kHz WAV audio stream from gemini-3.8-flash-lite-tts (using the Kore voice with a warm public-radio announcer style) and automatically falls back to browser SpeechSynthesisUtterance mapped to the exact Indian locale (en-IN, hi-IN, ta-IN, bn-IN, te-IN).

## 8. Error handling and edge cases

### Error handling and edge cases

1. Semantic Audit: Previously Half-Functional or Desynchronized Options Flagged & Fixed in Codebase
During a deep semantic inspection of the codebase, we identified five subtle interaction edge cases where controls were previously half-functional or left the UI temporarily out of sync, and engineered complete recovery paths for each:
Edge Case 1 — Scheme Card "Ask JanSahayak Guide" Context Handoff (src/App.tsx & src/components/JanSahayakChatView.tsx)
What Was Flagged: Previously, clicking the primary CTA on any scheme card in My Rights & Schemes switched the active tab to chat, but dropped the specific schemeTitle parameter (_schemeTitle), leaving the user on a generic chat screen without asking about the scheme they clicked.
Recovery & Fix Implemented: handleAskSahayak(schemeTitle) in src/App.tsx now stores a tailored query ("How do I apply step-by-step for ${schemeTitle}, what documents do I need, and how do I avoid middlemen?") in pendingChatQuestion and passes it into JanSahayakChatView via initialQuestion. A useEffect watcher in JanSahayakChatView.tsx immediately dispatches that exact question to /api/civic/chat upon tab mount and clears the pending state so the citizen gets an instant, scheme-specific legal walkthrough.
Edge Case 2 — Stale Output Card When Clicking Sample Notices or Reading Complexity Toggles (src/components/RightsAndSchemesView.tsx)
What Was Flagged: Previously, clicking one of the Try Official Sample Notice buttons (PM-KISAN Land Seeding, Municipal Street Vendor Notice, RTI Section 7(1) Order) or toggling between Simple Everyday Words and Detailed Legal Clauses updated the left-hand input state (documentText, uploadedImagePreview, readingLevel), but left the right-hand Plain-Language Translation card displaying the previous notice's analysis until the user manually clicked the submit button.
Recovery & Fix Implemented: handleLoadSampleNotice and both Reading Complexity toggle buttons now immediately invoke runDocumentExplanation(text, imagePreview, imageMime, level) with the newly selected values, ensuring the right-hand translation card is always semantically synchronized with the active sample and reading complexity level.
Edge Case 3 — Stale Verdict When Clicking Viral Claim Presets (src/components/FactCheckAuditView.tsx)
What Was Flagged: Clicking a 1-Tap Viral Claim Preset in Satya Fact-Check & Scam Shield previously populated the textarea and cleared the previous result to null, requiring a second manual click on Verify Claim & Detect Scam.
Recovery & Fix Implemented: Extracted executeAuditRequest(textToAudit, imgDataUrl, imgMime) and wired every preset button to trigger verification immediately on click so citizens see the updated Credibility Score and Scam Alert in a single tap.
Edge Case 4 — One-Way Audio Playback Buttons in Fact-Check & Chat (FactCheckAuditView.tsx & JanSahayakChatView.tsx)
What Was Flagged: While the top header and scheme cards had start/stop audio toggles, the Listen to Fact-Check button in FactCheckAuditView and the Read Aloud buttons on assistant messages in JanSahayakChatView previously only started speech and could not stop an active reading mid-sentence.
Recovery & Fix Implemented: Added isSpeakingAudit state in FactCheckAuditView.tsx and speakingMsgId state in JanSahayakChatView.tsx. Clicking an active audio button now invokes stopCivicSpeech(), immediately halting both HTML5 <audio> playback and window.speechSynthesis.
Edge Case 5 — Incomplete Scheme Coverage in Deterministic Offline Chat Fallback (server.ts)
What Was Flagged: In /api/civic/chat, the local statutory fallback originally had dedicated branches for Ration/NFSA, RTI, and Farmers, plus a generic message for other personas. If a Senior Citizen (70+), Street Vendor, Student, Woman Artisan, or Daily Wage Worker asked a question while the Gemini API was unreachable, they received a generic response instead of their exact statutory entitlement.
Recovery & Fix Implemented: Expanded the deterministic fallback engine in server.ts to cover all 6 citizen personas and all 6 flagship statutory schemes (Ayushman Vay Vandana 70+, PM SVANidhi & Street Vendors Act Sec 3(3), NSP Scholarships, PM Vishwakarma & Lakhpati Didi, e-Shram Accidental Cover, PM-KISAN, NFSA Ration, and RTI Act Sec 6(1)/7(1)).
2. Incomplete, Malformed, or Extreme Citizen Input Edge Cases
Empty Text & Missing Photo Submissions
Scenario: A user clicks Translate Notice into Plain Language, Verify Claim & Detect Scam, or Send in JanSahayak Chat without typing text or attaching a photo.
Frontend Recovery: Each component checks (!text.trim() && !imagePreview) before making a network call. In RightsAndSchemesView and FactCheckAuditView, an inline high-contrast alert banner (role="alert") instructs the user to enter text, pick a sample preset, or upload a document photo. In JanSahayakChatView, the Send button is disabled (disabled={!input.trim() && !attachedImage}) to prevent empty turns.
Backend Guardrail: If a raw POST request reaches /api/civic/explain-document without documentText or imageBase64, server.ts does not crash or return an unhandled error; instead, it defaults to analyzing the statutory PM-KISAN circular so a valid structured JSON schema is always returned.
Oversized Camera Photos (10MB+ High-Resolution Mobile Captures) or Non-Image Files
Scenario: A citizen uploads a raw 12-megapixel smartphone photo (which would exceed Express's 15mb JSON payload limit once base64-encoded or cause slow mobile uploads) or selects a non-image file (e.g., .pdf or .exe).
Recovery Method (processUploadedImageFile in src/utils/speechHelper.ts):
Validates file.type.startsWith('image/') and rejects files over 15MB with a clear human-readable message.
Loads the image into an offscreen HTML5 <canvas>, proportionally downscales any dimension exceeding 1200px, and re-encodes it as a compressed image/jpeg (0.82 quality) data URL.
If the browser fails to decode a corrupted image header, the promise rejects cleanly and displays an inline error banner without breaking component state.
Extreme or Out-of-Bounds Demographic Numbers (RightsAndSchemesView.tsx & civicLogic.ts)
Scenario: A user clears the Age or Annual Family Income input field (producing NaN), enters a negative income, or enters an age above 120.
Recovery Method: Input onChange handlers sanitize values via Number(e.target.value) || 0 (or default age 35) with HTML min/max bounds (min={5}, max={105} for age; min={0} for income). In evaluateSchemeEligibility(profile), every rule checks explicit numeric bounds and returns both eligible schemes and ineligible schemes along with a populated missingRequirements array explaining why a scheme is locked (e.g., "Requires Senior Citizen aged 70+ (Current age: 42)" or "Annual household income (₹3,50,000) exceeds ₹2,00,000 limit").
3. Three-Tier AI Model & Network Failure Cascade (server.ts)
Every AI route on the server (/api/civic/explain-document, /api/civic/fact-check, /api/civic/chat) implements a 3-tier resilience cascade so that API rate limits, model unavailability, malformed JSON, or offline conditions never expose a 500 error or blank state:
Tier 1 — Primary Model (gemini-3.8-flash) with Strict Schema / Grounding
Calls ai.models.generateContent using gemini-3.8-flash with responseMimeType: 'application/json' and a strict responseSchema (or tools: [{ googleSearch: {} }] on /api/civic/fact-check).
Tier 2 — Automatic Model Fallback (gemini-flash-latest)
Our generateWithFlashFallback wrapper in server.ts catches any model-level error on gemini-3.8-flash and automatically retries the exact multimodal/schema request against gemini-flash-latest.
Tier 3 — Deterministic Statutory Engine Fallback (src/utils/civicLogic.ts & server.ts)
If GEMINI_API_KEY is missing, quota is exhausted, or the network is unreachable:
/api/civic/explain-document inspects the notice text for statutory keywords (vending, svanidhi, rti, section 7, kisan, ration) and returns a complete, schema-compliant 3-step explanation with modelUsed: 'deterministic-civic-fallback'.
/api/civic/fact-check runs evaluateClaimCredibilityHeuristics(claimText) from src/utils/civicLogic.ts, which scans for scam/phishing indicators (whatsapp, click link, processing fee, apk, otp, instant loan) versus verified statutory domains (70+, ayushman, pm-kisan, nfsa), returning a deterministic credibilityScore (14% for scams, 96% for verified statutory provisions, 58% for unverified rumors), a verdict, and a scamWarning.
/api/civic/chat matches the citizen's question and profile occupation against the 8-domain statutory knowledge base and returns a complete answer, actionChecklist, statutoryCitation, and deterministic receiptHash.
4. Multimodal Voice & Audio Synthesis Recovery Chain (src/utils/speechHelper.ts)
Because low-literacy citizens rely on voice output in 5 languages (en, hi, ta, bn, te), audio playback has a dual-engine fallback with timeout protection:
Primary Engine — Server-Side Gemini Neural TTS (/api/civic/tts using gemini-3.8-flash-lite-tts)
speakCivicText sends the text (capped at 600 characters to prevent synthesis latency) to /api/civic/tts with an AbortController timeout of 5,500ms.
Converts raw 24kHz 16-bit mono PCM bytes from Gemini into a valid RIFF/WAV header in browser memory (pcmBase64ToWavUrl) and plays it via HTML5 Audio.
Automatic Fallback — Browser Web Speech Synthesis (window.speechSynthesis)
If /api/civic/tts returns { fallbackToBrowserSpeech: true }, times out after 5.5 seconds, or if browser autoplay policies reject <audio>.play(), speakCivicText seamlessly falls back to speakWithBrowserSynthesis(text, language) mapped to regional BCP-47 locales (en-IN, hi-IN, ta-IN, bn-IN, te-IN).
Overlap & Concurrency Prevention
Calling speakCivicText or stopCivicSpeech always cancels any existing activeAudio instance and calls window.speechSynthesis.cancel() first, preventing two voices from talking over each other if a user rapidly clicks multiple Listen buttons.
5. Browser Permission & Hardware Capability Edge Cases
Unsupported Voice Dictation (SpeechRecognition / webkitSpeechRecognition in JanSahayakChatView.tsx)
Scenario: A user clicks the Voice Input microphone button on a desktop browser (such as Firefox) or an iFrame context where Web Speech Recognition is unavailable or microphone permission is denied.
Recovery Method: Instead of failing silently or throwing a ReferenceError, toggleVoiceInput checks for constructor availability and wraps recognition.start() in try/catch + onerror. If unavailable or blocked, it populates the chat input box with a helpful sample question ("How can I file a free RTI application if my ration or pension is delayed?") and shows an inline amber notice explaining how to edit or send the query via text.
Blocked Clipboard API in Sandboxed iFrames (JanSahayakChatView.tsx)
Scenario: A user generates a statutory Section 6(1) RTI Application Draft and clicks Copy RTI Text, or clicks Download Official RTI Draft (.txt).
Recovery Method: Provide both navigator.clipboard?.writeText(rtiOutput.draftText) (with a visual "Copied to Clipboard" confirmation state) and a direct Blob download (RTI_Application_${trackingId}.txt) so the citizen can always save their legal draft even if clipboard permissions are restricted by the browser environment.

## 9. Safety, privacy, constraints, and integrations

### Safety, privacy, constraints, integrations

1. What Must Be Protected (Citizen Privacy, Data Minimization & Credential Security)
Zero Persistent Storage of Sensitive Citizen Identity (PII & Documents)
Citizens using JanSetu often upload photos of land records (Khasra/Khatauni), ration rejection slips, hospital bills, or legal notices, and enter household income or age to check welfare eligibility.
Stateless Ephemeral Processing: All demographic profile state (CitizenProfile in src/App.tsx) lives exclusively in client-side memory for the active session. When a document photo or notice is sent to /api/civic/explain-document, /api/civic/fact-check, or /api/civic/chat, the base64 payload is processed ephemerally in RAM (server.ts) and passed to Gemini for immediate inference—never written to disk, logged in plaintext, or stored in an external database.
Protection Against Identity Theft & Aadhaar/OTP Harvesting
Rural and low-literacy citizens are frequent targets of middlemen and WhatsApp phishing scams asking for 12-digit Aadhaar numbers, bank PINs, or OTPs.
JanSetu never asks citizens to input their Aadhaar number, bank account number, or mobile OTP. Instead, it tells them which documents to carry to an authorized Common Service Centre (CSC) or official .gov.in portal, and explicitly reminds them in every scheme card and RTI draft that official enrollment fees are ₹0 (or exempted under RTI Rule 3 for BPL families).
Strict Server-Side API Key & Secret Isolation
The GEMINI_API_KEY is accessed strictly inside the Express backend (server.ts via process.env.GEMINI_API_KEY). No API keys, tokens, or credentials are ever bundled into client-side Vite JavaScript or exposed in browser network headers.
2. What Must Be Avoided (Anti-Hallucination, Legal Guardrails & Dark Patterns)
Avoiding Unverified Legal or Welfare Hallucinations
In civic governance, inventing an eligibility threshold or benefit amount can waste a daily-wage worker's day at a government office.
Deterministic Eligibility & Statutory Grounding: Core entitlement calculations (evaluateSchemeEligibility), RTI Section 6(1)/7(1) legal notice formatting (generateRTIDraft), and scam heuristic scoring (evaluateClaimCredibilityHeuristics) are governed by deterministic rules in src/utils/civicLogic.ts anchored to real Indian statutes (NFSA 2013, RTI Act 2005, Street Vendors Act 2014, PM-KISAN, Ayushman Bharat 70+, e-Shram).
Structured Schema Enforcement: When Gemini Flash translates a circular (/api/civic/explain-document) or answers a legal query (/api/civic/chat), server.ts enforces a strict JSON responseSchema requiring an explicit statutoryAuthority or statutoryCitation field and anti-scam fee warnings.
Avoiding Financial Phishing, APK Malware, and Middleman Exploitation
The Satya Fact-Check & Scam Shield (FactCheckAuditView.tsx + /api/civic/fact-check) actively scans claims for high-risk fraud vectors—such as .apk downloads, non-.gov.in domains, "processing fee" requests, and WhatsApp lottery/subsidy links—and surfaces a prominent Scam & Fraud Protection Alert box telling users what not to click or share.
Avoiding Accessibility Barriers, Complex Jargon, and Intrusive Browser Popups
No Legal Jargon by Default: Explanations default to Simple Everyday Words (6th-grade reading level) with a 3-step action checklist.
Sandbox & iFrame Safety: Avoids window.alert(), window.confirm(), or blocked popup flows; all validation errors, RTI copy confirmations, and voice fallback notifications render as accessible inline status banners (role="alert").
3. What Must Be Supported (Technical Constraints & Multimodal Integrations)
Low-Bandwidth & Entry-Level Smartphone Constraints
Client-Side Image Compression (processUploadedImageFile in src/utils/speechHelper.ts): High-resolution mobile camera photos (up to 15MB) are validated and downscaled on an offscreen HTML5 <canvas> to a maximum dimension of 1200px (image/jpeg at 0.82 quality) before base64 transmission, keeping payloads fast and well under Express's 15mb body limit.
Offline / Degraded Network Resilience: Every endpoint supports a 3-tier execution cascade (gemini-3.8-flash 
 gemini-flash-latest 
 deterministic statutory engine fallback), ensuring the app remains 100% functional even if upstream AI quota or internet connectivity drops.
Multilingual & Voice-First Accessibility (5 Indian Languages)
Full UI localization and voice readout across English (en-IN), Hindi (hi-IN), Tamil (ta-IN), Bengali (bn-IN), and Telugu (te-IN).
Dual-Layer Speech Integration: Integrates server-side Gemini Neural TTS (gemini-3.8-flash-lite-tts returning 24kHz PCM audio wrapped into an in-memory WAV container) with a 5.5-second timeout that automatically falls back to browser window.speechSynthesis in the matching Indian regional locale.
Core External & AI Integrations Supported
@google/genai SDK (gemini-3.8-flash & gemini-flash-latest): Powers multimodal vision OCR on photographed government notices/screenshots, structured JSON policy translation, and multilingual legal guidance via /api/civic/explain-document and /api/civic/chat.
Google Search Grounding (tools: [{ googleSearch: {} }]): Integrated into /api/civic/fact-check so viral claims and scheme rumors are cross-checked against live public web sources (such as Press Information Bureau and official ministry notifications), returning clickable source citations alongside the verdict.
Web Speech & Clipboard APIs: Supports browser voice dictation (SpeechRecognition / webkitSpeechRecognition with graceful text preset fallback) and 1-click RTI application export via both navigator.clipboard and .txt Blob file download.
